N°01 — About Me

Where business thinking meets technical execution.

I'm a Business Information Technology student at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology, Cambodia, where I sit at the intersection of two worlds that most people treat as separate: business strategy and software engineering. I believe the most valuable technologists are the ones who understand why a solution matters to a business before they decide how to build it.

My curiosity started with spreadsheets and business models, then grew into web development, database design, and data visualization — because I kept running into the same question: how do you turn a plan into something people can actually use? That question still drives everything I build today.

I approach every project with a mix of structured problem solving, continuous learning, and collaboration. Whether I'm leading a university team project, analyzing a marketing campaign, or designing an interface, I care about clarity, craftsmanship, and outcomes that make sense for the business behind them.
